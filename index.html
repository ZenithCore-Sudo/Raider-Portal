    Raider Portal

    Version 2.6 · Made by Damien Mehlbaum

    A student hub for Middletown Area High School with apps, tools, a calendar and school info.

    New in 2.6Everything below is new in this version.
    PlannerA new tab with stat tiles and a progress bar. Assignments are grouped by due date, with subject filters, sorting, quick date buttons, priorities and inline editing.
    ToolsStats calculator, GCF and LCM, base converter, password maker, text tools, Roman numerals, and a full color picker with palettes.
    ThemesMidnight, Neon, Lavender, Arctic, Dracula, Nord, Solar and Terminal.
    SettingsAccent color picker, greeting line, scroll bar, blur, rounded corners, card hover lift, large tap targets and high contrast borders.
    ShortcutsRandom app, scroll to top, Planner, focus mode, and next and previous tab. All can be rebound below.
    ContactsCounseling and School info cards now show formatted phone numbers with Copy buttons, tappable emails and a Send email button.
